About ZELVO

ZELVO is an online shopping platform designed to provide customers with a simple and convenient shopping experience. Browse products, add them to your cart and place your order directly through the website.

Privacy Policy

ZELVO stores information entered by customers in this browser for the purpose of this website's local order system. Customers should avoid entering sensitive information that is not required for an order.

Terms & Conditions

Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact and delivery information. Product prices and availability may be updated by ZELVO. Orders can be cancelled according to their current status.

Shipping & Delivery

Orders are processed after being placed. Delivery time can vary according to the destination and delivery service. Customers should provide a correct address and active mobile number.

Returns & Refunds

For eligible damaged or incorrect orders, customers should contact ZELVO with their Order ID. Returns and refunds are subject to the store's applicable return policy.

Contact ZELVO

For order-related questions, please keep your Order ID available when contacting ZELVO.
